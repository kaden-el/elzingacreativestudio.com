Common Questions

Social Media Marketing FAQ — Grand Rapids

What social media marketing services do you offer?

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), Google Ads, Google Business Profile optimization, and content creation. Services can be taken individually or bundled — most clients start with GBP optimization and one ad channel, then expand from there.

Do you create the ad creative or just manage the campaigns?

Both. We shoot the photo and video content and run the campaigns. Having one team do both means the creative is built specifically for ad performance — sized right, message right, visual right. You're not sending us assets you found on your phone.

How does Google Business Profile optimization help my business?

GBP is what determines whether you show up in the map pack when someone searches "landscaper near me" or "Grand Rapids contractor." Optimizing your categories, service areas, photos, and review strategy is one of the highest-ROI things a local business can do — and most businesses have it set up wrong.

How do you measure results?

Leads, calls, and form fills — not follower counts or impressions. We send a clear monthly report showing what the budget produced. If a campaign isn't working, we tell you and we fix it.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Landscapers, contractors, home service companies, restaurants, and local service businesses across West Michigan. We focus on businesses where local visibility directly drives revenue — not e-commerce or national brands.

What areas do you serve?

Grand Rapids and all of West Michigan — Wyoming, Kentwood, Walker, Grandville, East Grand Rapids, Ada, Cascade, Caledonia, Byron Center, Forest Hills, Rockford, Lowell, Jenison, Hudsonville, Holland, Zeeland, Coopersville, and beyond across Kent and Ottawa counties. Digital marketing can run from anywhere, but we prefer working with local businesses we can actually visit and shoot content for.
