Sheet 03/ The marking layer

Red marks decisions.

The keeper circle (text only), the cull dim, the pick outline, the frame plate — one color, four jobs, zero decoration. Nothing is ever drawn over a photograph.

Keeper mark — .ez-keep (headline word)

Shoot everything.
Keep the keepers.

The cull — .ez-cull (frame dims; no marks over photos)
Frame plate — .ez-plate (image caption)
Marked word — h3 <em> underline

Windows balanced. Sky real.

Usage

Red marks decisions only — a keeper circle around a word, a pick outline around a surviving frame, a CTA. It is never drawn across a photograph. It never appears as decoration, background, or emphasis for its own sake. If red isn't marking something a viewer needs to notice, it doesn't belong on the page.

Sheet 05/ Misuse

Don't do this.

Every one of these has shipped somewhere before. Not again.

  • ✕Warm cream backgrounds — Edition 03 paper is cool print-white, not the old bone.
  • ✕Italic accent words in headlines — the marked word is upright with a red underline, never italic.
  • ✕Rounded corners past 3px — prints aren't rounded; radius caps at 2px everywhere.
  • ✕Red as wallpaper or a large fill — red marks a decision, it never covers a surface.
  • ✕Grain, marquee, scramble, tilt, or cursor effects — killed forever in Edition 03.
  • ✕Faked frame numbers — every plate is a real filename from the shoot, never invented.
  • ✕Urgency copy ("book now before...") — the voice states facts; it doesn't pressure.
