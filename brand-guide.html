A Studio Note
On the system
& how to use it

This is not a rulebook to trap us — it is a set of decisions made once, so we don't re-litigate them on every project. The wordmark, the dot, the bone paper and the single accent are fixed. Everything else is judgement, applied with care.

Used well, the system should feel invisible — the work looks unmistakably ours without ever shouting about it. When in doubt, choose the quieter option and let the typography carry the weight.

01

The Mark

The wordmark is set in Syne with a single accent period — the "dot" — that anchors the identity. It works as a full wordmark, a stacked lockup, or a standalone monogram.

04

Voice

We write the way we design — direct, warm and unafraid of a strong opinion. Plain words, real craft, no jargon.

We say"A logo that earns its place."
Not"Synergistic brand-forward solutions."
We say"Let's make it sharper."
Not"Let's circle back and ideate."
05

Principles

01
Tension over noise

One bold move beats ten timid ones. We design with contrast and conviction.

02
Craft is the message

Spacing, kerning and rhythm carry the brand as much as the logo does.

03
Warm, not soft

Bone paper and a single accent keep the system human while staying decisive.

04
Built to flex

The mark, the dot and the palette adapt across every surface without losing identity.
