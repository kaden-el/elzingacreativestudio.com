Why Cascade listings are a luxury market

Cascade Township sits in Kent County as one of the most affluent suburbs southeast of Grand Rapids, about 10 to 12 miles from downtown. The Thornapple River winds through it, threading past Thornapple Country Club and a stretch of riverfront and wooded executive lots, and the township is anchored by top-rated Forest Hills Public Schools. The result is a market built on higher-end and executive homes, newer luxury subdivisions, and the village along Cascade Road. Drone in Cascade airspace

Cascade has an airspace detail most photographers miss: Gerald R. Ford International Airport (GRR) sits inside Cascade Township, so much of Cascade falls in controlled airspace, Class C or D, where a drone flight requires LAANC authorization. ECS flies Part 107 RPIC compliant and pulls LAANC ahead of the shoot, so the flight is legal and the shoot stays on schedule rather than getting grounded on site. For the full rundown, see drone laws for Grand Rapids real estate agents. Travel is free within roughly 25 miles of downtown Grand Rapids, which comfortably covers Cascade.
