Common Questions

Referral Program FAQ.

Who can refer?

Any past or current ECS client. If you have ever booked a paid session with us — listing, portrait, brand, senior, wedding, commercial — you are eligible. New referrers can stack credit from day one.

What counts as a paid session?

Any booking that results in money changing hands. Includes listings ($245 and up), portraits, headshots, brand sessions, senior sessions, weddings, and commercial retainers. Not eligible: free audits, consults, or no-show bookings.

Is there a cap?

No cap. Refer one client, get $50 off your next session. Refer ten clients, save $500. Credits stack until you use them.

How fast does the credit post?

Within 7 days of the referred client's first paid session completing. We confirm by email and the credit attaches to your next invoice automatically.

Can a realtor refer another realtor?

Yes — this is one of the most common cases. Real estate runs on word of mouth, and the Preferred Photographer Program is built around this exact loop. Refer an agent, get $50 off your next listing.

What if the referred person was already in conversation with you?

If they were actively quoted or scheduled before you referred, the credit does not apply. If they had only heard the name in passing, it does. We confirm attribution with the referred client at first contact — we will not double-claim.

Why not pay cash?

Two reasons. First, cash commissions on real estate referrals can run into RESPA issues — credit on services we provide does not. Second, this model rewards clients who actually use ECS, which keeps the program tied to people who know our work.
