At this price band, real twilight and drone aerial are not premium add-ons. They are baseline professional. Drone aerial — what to expect in Forest Hills airspace

Forest Hills and most of Cascade Township sit in Class E airspace, which is friendlier than the Class C controlled airspace around downtown Grand Rapids. Most flights do not require LAANC authorization. Egypt Valley CC and Cascade Hills CC have additional considerations (golf course operations, member privacy) that ECS handles through pre-shoot coordination. For the full breakdown on drone laws and Part 107 compliance for West Michigan real estate, see drone laws for Grand Rapids real estate agents.
