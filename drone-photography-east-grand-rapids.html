Why East GR listings need aerial

East Grand Rapids is a high-design, high-expectation in-town market, and the buyer is higher-income, design-aware buyers who expect an MLS gallery to look like a magazine. East GR is a lot-and-location market, and aerial is the only way to show how a home sits between Reeds Lake, the Gaslight commercial core, and the walkable street grid.

An ECS aerial set is not one hero shot. It is a front elevation from altitude, a lot-line frame that shows the parcel honestly, the context frame that places the home in East GR, and the detail passes the property actually calls for — roofline, outbuildings, water, tree cover. They are shot on the same visit as the stills and land in the same gallery.

Flying legally over East GR

ECS flies Part 107 certificated on every shoot, carries the registration on the aircraft, and checks the airspace over the address before the shoot date. Where an address falls inside controlled airspace, a LAANC authorization is filed before the flight, and we handle the filing.
