Why Jenison listings have a different buyer profile

Jenison is the suburb families pick when they want a quiet, established community with fast access to both Grand Rapids and the lakeshore. Sitting in Georgetown Township in Ottawa County, just west of Grandville and Wyoming, it is a true commuter-family market: the buyer is usually a household choosing schools and a daily drive, not a downtown lifestyle. Listings that win here lead with school district, family space, and the ease of the commute.

Elzinga Creative Studio shoots Jenison listings with attention to what these buyers actually weigh: the well-regarded Jenison Public Schools zone, functional family layouts, yard and garage space, and how quickly you can reach the I-196 / Chicago Drive corridor toward Grand Rapids one way and Lake Michigan the other. Drone aerials show the neighborhood feel and how the home sits in Jenison's street grid.

Drone in Jenison airspace

ECS flies Part 107 RPIC compliant on every Jenison shoot. Jenison sits west of Gerald R. Ford International Airport, and parts of the area fall under the controlled-airspace shelf tied to that airport, so we check the UAS grid and pull LAANC authorization before the flight whenever a grid cell requires it. That is handled before we arrive, so the aerial happens on schedule. Drone is included in Signature and a $125 add-on on Essential or Showcase.
