Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Elzinga Creative Studio ("ECS," "we," "us," or "our") operates elzingacreativestudio.com. This page explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Fill out our contact or booking form (name, email address, phone number, message)
  • Email or call us directly

We do not use tracking cookies, ad pixels, or third-party analytics on this website. We do not collect any information automatically beyond what your browser sends in a standard HTTP request.

How We Use Your Information

Information you submit through our contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule sessions, send invoices, and deliver your gallery. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Email Communications

If you contact us, we may follow up by email regarding your project. You can opt out of any non-essential communications at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or contacting us directly.

Third-Party Services

We deliver photo galleries through Pixieset. When you access a Pixieset gallery, Pixieset's own privacy policy applies to that interaction. We use Google Fonts to render typography on this site — Google may log the font request; see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Data Retention

We retain contact information for as long as needed to complete your project and for reasonable follow-up (typically no longer than two years). You may request deletion of your information at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at:

  • Email: kaden@elzingacreativestudio.com
  • Phone: (616) 258-4578

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at kaden@elzingacreativestudio.com or call (616) 258-4578.
