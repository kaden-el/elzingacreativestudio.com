Common Questions

Brand Day FAQ.

What's included in the Brand Day Starter?

$2,500 flat: one full day on-site (4-6 hours), one 60-90 second hero video, three 15-30 second vertical Reels, twenty edited brand photos, all raw footage, and one revision round. Built for a single business / single location.

How fast do I get the final video and photos?

Photos in 5 business days. Reels in 7 business days. Hero video in 10 business days. One revision round is included on the hero — additional revisions billed at $150/hr.

What if I need drone footage?

Add the $400 drone package. Part 107 licensed pilot, 1-2 aerial shots for your hero video, and extra B-roll. Adds about 45 minutes to the shoot day.

Who is this designed for?

Coaches, consultants, service-business owners, financial advisors, and small business owners who need a professional content library without the multi-shoot, multi-month timeline. One day, one flat fee, content you can actually use.

Can I book a Brand Day for multiple locations or a multi-person team?

The standard Brand Day is 1 day / 1 location / 1-2 key people. Multi-location, full-team, or product-line shoots are custom — we will quote a Brand Days Plus rate based on scope. Email kaden@elzingacreativestudio.com to scope.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancel 7+ days out: full deposit refund minus a $150 planning fee. Cancel 3-7 days out: 50% of deposit refunded. Cancel under 3 days: deposit non-refundable. Reschedule within 7 days is no-charge.

How do I book?
