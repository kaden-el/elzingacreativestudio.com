What direction should the new site feel?

This is the biggest design decision — everything else flows from it. Pick the mood that fits Elzinga Creative Studio.

Cinematic & Editorial

Dark backgrounds, full-bleed imagery, large white type. Feels like a high-end creative studio — dramatic, exclusive, artistic. Strong contrast between images and UI. Think: film production house, fashion photographer.

Best for: Weddings, portraits, commercial. Signals premium and exclusive.

Magazine Editorial

Evolve the existing warm palette — but much more sophisticated. Asymmetric layouts, editorial photo placement, strong typographic hierarchy. Feels like a premium lifestyle magazine. Warm and approachable but clearly high-craft.

Best for: All services. Builds on existing brand recognition and feels natural for GR market.

Bold & Minimal

Stark white, heavy sans-serif type, high contrast. Very contemporary — more agency than photographer. Clean, confident, a little aggressive. Works well if you want to pitch SMMA as seriously as photography.

Best for: SMMA-forward positioning. Signals modern agency, less traditional.
