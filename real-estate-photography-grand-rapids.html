Real Estate Photography FAQ — Grand Rapids

I haven't worked with you before. How do I know the photos will be good?

Fair question. Every shoot is the same photographer (Kaden), the same clean true-to-life editing style, and the same 24-hour turnaround, so your listings look consistent, not like a roster of strangers. Want to see recent work first? Text me at (616) 258-4578 and I’ll send a full gallery before you book a thing.

How fast will I get my real estate photos?

All packages include 24-hour delivery. Your MLS-ready images are professionally edited and delivered the next day via Pixieset so you can get your listing live fast.

Do you shoot real twilight or virtual twilight?

Real blue-hour twilight — not virtual edits. We schedule the shoot to hit the actual golden window at dusk. According to real estate industry research, twilight listings receive significantly more online views than daytime-only shots — some studies put the lift as high as 300%.

Is drone photography available?

Yes. Licensed Part 107 aerial drone is a $125 add-on on Essential and Showcase, and it’s included in Signature. Especially impactful for large lots, waterfront, acreage, and listings where neighborhood context matters.

Do you offer 3D virtual tours?

Yes. We shoot both Zillow 3D Home and Matterport-style walkthroughs as a $100 add-on to any photo package, captured during the same shoot as your stills and delivered MLS-ready. For higher-end and luxury listings, a premium fully-hosted Matterport Pro tour runs $250–$295, custom-quoted to the property.

What areas do you cover for real estate photography?

All of West Michigan — Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Kentwood, Walker, Grandville, East Grand Rapids, Ada, Cascade, Caledonia, Byron Center, Forest Hills, Lowell, Rockford, Belmont, Jenison, Hudsonville, Holland, Zeeland, Coopersville, and throughout Kent and Ottawa counties. No travel fee for listings within approximately 25 miles of downtown Grand Rapids.

How much does real estate photography cost?

Essential starts at $245 — HDR interiors and exteriors, 25–35 images, 24-hour delivery. Showcase is $375 and adds real twilight, a 2D floor plan, and social graphics. Signature is $525 and includes everything plus golden hour exteriors, Part 107 drone, and a 3D walkthrough tour (Zillow 3D Home).

What happens if the weather doesn't cooperate for twilight or drone?

We reschedule the twilight or drone portion at no charge — I’d rather wait a day for a real blue-hour sky than hand you a flat one. Interiors and daytime exteriors are still delivered on the original 24-hour timeline.

I list a lot. Do you do volume or team pricing?

Yes — if you’re listing regularly, we set up priority booking and a standing rate so every listing looks consistent and gets the same 24-hour turnaround. Text me at (616) 258-4578 and we’ll build it around your volume.
