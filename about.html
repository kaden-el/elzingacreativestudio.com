Background

Craft first.
Always.

Elzinga Creative Studio is built on a straightforward standard: nothing inherently wrong in the frame. Not trendy. Not over-processed. Just clean light, honest composition, and work that holds up when a potential buyer or client looks at it for the third time.

I started shooting real estate because it sits at the intersection of something I care about — making things look exactly as good as they are — and a market that has a real, measurable need for it. A listing with strong photography sells faster and for more money. That's not marketing copy, it's a documented statistical reality. I take that seriously.

Beyond real estate, I shoot senior portraits, weddings, commercial brand content, and sports work across West Michigan. Past commercial clients include Biggby Coffee, the Grand Rapids Griffins, and Mars Hill. Each project is different, but the standard is the same: the final image should look as good as the subject actually is, and nothing that's fixable should be left wrong.

I also run the digital marketing side of ECS — managing Meta and Google Ads campaigns, Google Business Profile optimization, and content strategy for local businesses. Most of my marketing clients are landscapers, contractors, and home service companies who do excellent work but whose online presence doesn't reflect it yet. That gap is fixable.

ECS is based in Grand Rapids, Michigan and serves clients throughout Kent and Ottawa counties — and beyond for the right project.
