Why Hastings listings have a different buyer profile

Hastings is the Barry County seat, a small city built along the Thornapple River about 30 miles southeast of Grand Rapids, with a genuine historic downtown wrapped around the State Street courthouse square and Charlton Park just outside town. The market is more varied than its size suggests: downtown and in-city move-up homes, riverfront on the Thornapple, lake homes on the Barry County lakes to the west, and rural acreage for buyers who want room. The buyer pool is local move-up, lake-home buyers, and Grand Rapids commuters trading drive time for acreage and value.

Elzinga Creative Studio shoots Hastings listings around what each of those buyers searches. For the in-city move-up home, it is warm, well-lit HDR and the walkable downtown feel. For riverfront and lake homes, it is drone and real twilight, because the water is the value and a still photo at golden hour is what makes a lake listing stop the scroll. Lake and riverfront homes carry a premium, and the photography has to earn it. Agents who list across Barry County often become a preferred photographer partner so every Hastings listing ships fast at a standing rate.

Drone in Hastings airspace

Hastings and the surrounding Barry County lakes sit in Class G uncontrolled airspace, the friendliest tier for commercial drone work, so most riverfront and lake listings need no LAANC authorization at all. ECS flies Part 107 RPIC compliant on every shoot and checks airspace ahead of time so the flight is legal and on schedule. Drone matters here because the water relationship on a river or lake home is the value, and you can only show it from the air.
