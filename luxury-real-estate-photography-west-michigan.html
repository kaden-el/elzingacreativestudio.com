Marketing that matches the price point

A $1.4M lakefront estate and a starter home do not sell the same way, and they should not be photographed the same way. At the top of West Michigan's market, buyers are not scrolling for square footage, they are buying a lifestyle, an address, and architecture. When a luxury listing goes live with the same flat phone snaps as everything else in the MLS, the price point and the marketing fall out of sync, and a buyer notices in the first frame. Luxury listing photography is not a daytime shoot with a markup, it is a different production built to do the property justice.

Elzinga Creative Studio shoots West Michigan's high-end listings as the full premium package: magazine-grade architectural photography, real twilight, drone aerials, a cinematic video walkthrough, and a Matterport 3D tour, delivered as one cohesive marketing set that makes a seven-figure home read like the asset it is. If you work the luxury tier regularly, becoming a preferred photographer partner keeps every estate listing on standing pricing and a priority calendar.

Drone, aerial, and real twilight

On a waterfront or estate listing, the setting is the value, and you can only show it from the air. Drone aerials make the property legible at a glance: the water frontage, the shoreline, and how the home sits on its ground. Drone is a $125 add-on. Twilight is the other half of the luxury look, included in scope on Signature. A real twilight is an actual blue-hour shoot, warm interior light glowing against a deep sky, and it is consistently the frame that stops a buyer mid-scroll. For why it pulls its weight, see do twilight photos sell homes, or the dedicated twilight photography page. ECS flies Part 107 RPIC compliant and pulls LAANC authorization in controlled airspace, so lakeshore and metro flights stay legal.

Cinematic video and Matterport 3D

Luxury buyers are often out of market, buying from another state before they ever stand in the home, which is why the premium package leans on motion and 3D. A cinematic video walkthrough paces a buyer through the spaces, views, and amenities, telling the lifestyle story a grid of stills cannot. A Matterport 3D tour lets a relocating buyer walk the whole floor plan on demand and understand the flow before booking a private showing. Together they pre-qualify serious interest and protect the seller's time. Standard photo delivery is within 24 hours, MLS-ready, via Pixieset; video and 3D follow shortly after.

Luxury listing FAQ

What makes a real estate shoot luxury-grade? It is the package, not a markup. A luxury listing gets the Signature tier at $495 — magazine-grade architectural photography with real twilight included in scope — then drone ($125), a cinematic video walkthrough, and a Matterport 3D tour ($100) added on so the marketing matches the price point. Real twilight is an actual blue-hour shoot, not a daytime frame faked dark.

Do you photograph Lake Michigan waterfront and estate properties? Yes. The lakeshore corridor is the heart of this work, from Macatawa and Holland to Saugatuck, Spring Lake, Grand Haven, and South Haven, plus affluent metro pockets like Ada, Cascade, East Grand Rapids, Forest Hills, and Reeds Lake. These listings sell on lifestyle, amenity, and architecture, so drone and twilight do a lot of the work.

How discreet is the process for high-net-worth sellers? Discretion is standard. We work around the seller's schedule, keep the shoot tight and professional, and never publish a property without the agent's say-so. The agent controls what gets used.

How fast is delivery? Photos are delivered within 24 hours of the shoot, MLS-ready, via Pixieset. Video and Matterport 3D follow shortly after. For a premium listing where time on market matters, that gets the gallery live fast without cutting quality.
