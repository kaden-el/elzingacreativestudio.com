Standale is the west-side gateway, and it sells to two buyers at once

Standale is the commercial and residential heart of Walker's west side, strung along Lake Michigan Drive (M-45) where the city hands off to the GVSU corridor and the open country toward Allendale. It is a practical, established market — and it serves two very different buyers at the same time. There's the owner-occupant family drawn to mature neighborhoods, the Kenowa Hills schools, and a quick west-side commute; and there's the investor or commuter buying near Grand Valley State University for the rental demand. A Standale listing often has to speak to both, and that shapes how it should be photographed.

Elzinga Creative Studio shoots Standale listings for whichever buyer the home is aimed at. For family listings, that means bright HDR interiors and clean exteriors that show off the established-neighborhood feel. For rentals and investment properties, it means the same professional-grade work that helps a unit lease faster and hold a stronger rent. Either way the gallery is back within 24 hours, MLS-ready and sized for Zillow.
