Common Questions

Senior Portrait Photography FAQ — Grand Rapids

How long is a senior portrait session?

Sessions start at 30+ minutes. Extended sessions are available for multiple outfit changes or multiple locations — reach out for a custom quote.

Where do you shoot senior portraits in Grand Rapids?

Anywhere you want — Riverside Park, Millennium Park, downtown Grand Rapids, your high school, a family farm, or any location that's meaningful to you. We shoot senior portraits throughout West Michigan: Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Kentwood, Walker, Grandville, East Grand Rapids, Ada, Cascade, Caledonia, Byron Center, Forest Hills, Rockford, Lowell, Belmont, Jenison, Hudsonville, Holland, Zeeland, Coopersville, and beyond — at no extra charge.

How many photos will I receive?

Every session includes a minimum of 25 fully edited, high-resolution images delivered via Pixieset. You'll get print-ready files and social media crops in the same download.

When will I get my photos?

Your gallery is delivered within 5 business days of your session. You'll get an email with a Pixieset link to download, share, or order prints.

Can I bring a friend or sibling to my session?

Absolutely. We regularly shoot seniors with their best friend or a sibling. Just mention it when you book so we can plan the session accordingly.
