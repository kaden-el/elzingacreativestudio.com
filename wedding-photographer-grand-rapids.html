Common Questions

Wedding Photography FAQ — Grand Rapids & West Michigan

How far in advance should I book?

As early as possible — popular summer and fall dates fill quickly. Most couples book 6–12 months out. That said, if you have a date coming up, reach out and we'll see what we can make work.

Do you travel outside Grand Rapids?

Yes. We cover all of West Michigan — Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Kentwood, Walker, Grandville, East Grand Rapids, Ada, Cascade, Byron Center, Forest Hills, Rockford, Lowell, Hudsonville, Holland, Zeeland, and beyond. For venues outside our standard area (roughly 25 miles of downtown Grand Rapids), reach out with your venue location and we'll discuss travel details.

How many photos will we receive?

Every wedding is different, but a typical full-day package delivers 400–700+ fully edited images. You'll receive every worthwhile moment — we don't withhold images to sell prints.

When will we get our wedding photos?

Your gallery is delivered via Pixieset within 4–6 weeks of your wedding. You'll receive a link to download full-resolution files and share with family and friends.

What is your photography style?

Documentary and natural. We capture moments as they happen rather than stopping to stage and direct. You'll get real expressions, real emotion, and a real record of your day — not a catalog of posed portraits.
