Why video earns the listing more attention

A buyer scrolling the MLS or Zillow gives most listings a two-second glance. Video is what slows them down. A moving walkthrough lets a buyer feel the flow of a home in a way a grid of stills cannot, and that extra attention shows up as longer time-on-listing and more saves. Both the MLS and Zillow accept listing video, and Zillow actively surfaces listings that carry it, so a video is not a vanity add-on, it is a placement advantage. For a Grand Rapids agent competing for the listing, offering professional video is also a pitch tool, it is one of the easiest things to show a seller you do that the agent down the street does not.

Cinematic walkthrough vs. quick highlight reel

These are two different tools and it helps to be clear about which job each one does. A cinematic walkthrough is the longer horizontal video, usually a minute to two minutes, that flows through the home room by room with a drone establishing shot and your branded intro and outro. It lives on the MLS, Zillow, YouTube, and your website, and its job is to give a serious buyer the full feel of the property before they ever book a showing. It is the centerpiece video for the listing.

A quick highlight reel is the opposite end, a fast vertical cut of 15 to 45 seconds built to stop a thumb mid-scroll. It is not trying to show the whole house, it is trying to earn reach, saves, and a tap through to the full listing. Drone integrates into both: in the walkthrough it is the establishing open, in the reel it is often the hook in the first second. Many agents run the cinematic walkthrough on the portals and a couple of reels on social off the same visit, so one shoot feeds every channel.
