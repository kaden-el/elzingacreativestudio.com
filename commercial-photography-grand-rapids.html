Common Questions

Commercial Photography FAQ — Grand Rapids

What is a monthly content retainer?

A retainer is a recurring monthly package — we come to your job sites or business on a set schedule and photograph your work. You get a consistent stream of professional images for your website, Google Business Profile, and social media without having to think about it each month.

What kinds of businesses do you photograph?

Landscapers, contractors, home service companies, restaurants, retail stores, and professional service businesses across West Michigan. Past commercial clients include Biggby Coffee, the Grand Rapids Griffins, and Mars Hill. If your work looks better in person than it does on your phone, we can help.

Do you shoot before and after photos?

Yes — and it's one of the most valuable things we do. We photograph the project at start and completion so you have compelling comparison content for estimates, your website, and social media. Before/afters are the most shared content type in trades and home services.

How does the free demo shoot work?

We come out for one shoot at no cost so you can see exactly what the final product looks like before committing to a retainer. No obligation, no pressure. Most businesses that do a demo convert — because the difference between iPhone photos and professional ones is hard to unsee.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Our retainers are month-to-month. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you into something. Most clients stay because the content is working — not because they have to.

What areas do you serve?

All of West Michigan — Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Kentwood, Walker, Grandville, East Grand Rapids, Ada, Cascade, Caledonia, Byron Center, Forest Hills, Rockford, Lowell, Jenison, Hudsonville, Holland, Zeeland, Coopersville, and throughout Kent and Ottawa counties. No travel fee within approximately 25 miles of downtown Grand Rapids.
