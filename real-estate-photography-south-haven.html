Why South Haven listings sell to a buyer who is rarely in town

South Haven sits on the Lake Michigan lakeshore in Van Buren County, roughly 40 miles southwest of Grand Rapids. That makes it one of West Michigan's premier beach towns, and the market reflects it: second homes, beach cottages, vacation rentals, and waterfront estates dominate, with the Black River running through downtown to the harbor and marina. South Beach, North Beach, the pier and lighthouse, and the surrounding blueberry country (the town hosts the National Blueberry Festival) are the backdrop buyers are paying for. The listings split three clear ways. One is the walkable in-town cottage, one is Lake-Michigan or inland-lake waterfront, and one is the downtown condo — and the buyer for all three is frequently shopping from out of town, on a screen, before they ever drive in.

Elzinga Creative Studio shoots South Haven listings around how that buyer actually decides. Because so many are second-home and luxury buyers evaluating a property remotely, twilight, drone, and a strong gallery carry more weight here than almost anywhere else in West Michigan. A real twilight exterior sells the lakeside lifestyle; drone shows the water-and-dune relationship and how close the property sits to the harbor and the beach; clean, bright HDR interiors make a cottage or condo read move-in ready. In a hot seasonal market, fast 24-hour delivery gets the listing live while the buyer is still looking. If you list here often, it is worth becoming a preferred photographer partner so every South Haven listing ships fast at a standing rate.

Drone for the water-and-dune story

On a South Haven listing, the relationship between the home, the water, the dune, and the beach is the value — and you can only show it from the air. Drone aerials make that legible at a glance: how close the property sits to Lake Michigan or an inland lake, where the shoreline and beach access are, the proximity to the Black River harbor, the pier, and downtown, and how the lot sits in the dune landscape. For a second-home buyer comparing waterfront listings from out of town, that aerial is often what earns the showing. Drone is a $125 add-on, or built into the Showcase tier. Paired with a real twilight exterior, it is the single strongest way to sell the lakeshore lifestyle — see do twilight photos sell homes for why.

Drone in South Haven airspace

The lakeshore around South Haven is mostly Class G uncontrolled airspace — South Haven Area Regional Airport (LWA) is small and non-towered — which is the friendliest tier for commercial drone work. In most cases no LAANC authorization is needed, which keeps waterfront and harbor flights simple. ECS still checks airspace before every flight, flies Part 107 RPIC compliant on every shoot, and pulls LAANC in the rare case a listing falls in controlled airspace, so the flight is legal and the shoot stays on schedule. South Haven is beyond the roughly 25-mile free travel radius from Grand Rapids, so the slightly longer drive to the South Haven lakeshore is handled with a small travel arrangement, confirmed when you book.
