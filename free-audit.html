Common Questions

Free Listing Photo Audit FAQ.

Is the listing photo audit really free?

Yes. No card, no commitment, no recurring email pitch you can't unsubscribe from. We do this because most realtor listing photos in Grand Rapids have 2-3 easy fixes that would move their numbers, and an audit costs us ~30 minutes. If the audit is useful, you book your next listing with us. If it's not, you got a free critique.

What do I send you for the audit?

Pixieset link, Zillow URL, MLS gallery link, or a Dropbox/Drive folder of the photos. Active or recent listings only — we audit shots that are or were live.

What do I get back?

A 5-minute video critique walking through your gallery: which 3 shots are pulling down engagement, what changed compositions or lighting would have fixed them, and what 1-2 missing shots would have moved the listing faster.

How fast do I get the audit back?

48 hours from submission. We are a real West Michigan studio, not a content farm — first 5 audits each week, then waitlist.

Will you publish my listing or photos publicly?

Only with explicit permission. The form has a checkbox: yes/no on whether we can use the audit as a public case study with the listing address obscured. Default is off.

What's the catch?

The catch is honest: we do this as our top-funnel marketing. If the audit lands, you remember us next time you have a $300K+ listing where the photos matter. If it doesn't, you walk away with a free critique. We track captures, not commitments.

Do you audit listings outside West Michigan?

We focus on Kent and Ottawa counties. If you are a Grand Rapids agent listing in a neighboring market, send it — we will probably do it. National listings, we usually decline because we cannot follow up with a real shoot.
