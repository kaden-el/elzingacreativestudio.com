Common Questions

Preferred Photographer Program FAQ.

What is the Preferred Photographer Program?

A standing photography partnership for Grand Rapids real estate agents. We become your go-to on every listing — three tiers, real twilight, drone, 24-hour delivery, priority calendar access, and a $50 referral credit when you bring another agent in. You reach House Photographer status after your second paid shoot.

How is this different from Lakeshore Lens or other Grand Rapids real estate photographers?

Two things. First, our Showcase tier is $325 vs Lakeshore's $435 for similar scope — $110 cheaper per listing. Second, we shoot real blue-hour twilight, not virtual twilight edits. Real twilight listings consistently outperform daytime-only photos for online views.

Do I have to commit to a contract or minimum?

No. No retainer, no minimum, no exclusivity. The "Preferred Photographer" name describes the working relationship — you choose to use us repeatedly because it is consistent and easy, not because a contract obligates you.

What if I have a listing under $200K?

Essential at $245 is built for it. HDR interiors and exteriors, 25-35 MLS-ready images, 24-hour delivery. We do not push you up a tier on listings that do not need it.

Do you offer drone photography for real estate?

Yes. Part 107 licensed aerial drone shots are an add-on for Essential and Showcase, and included in Signature. Especially impactful for waterfront listings, large lots, and neighborhood context.

Can my brokerage set this up as a preferred-vendor program?

Yes — we work with brokerage offices in a RESPA-compliant way: co-marketing, free office content, lunch-and-learns, and volume rates. No kickbacks. If you would like to introduce us to your broker, text Kaden at (616) 258-4578 — we will put together a one-pager for the office.

What's in the $150 real twilight add-on?

A separate blue-hour shoot scheduled for the same listing — 20-40 minute window post-sunset, interior lights warm, exteriors lit. Get 5-8 additional twilight exteriors delivered with your main gallery. Most Showcase clients add it on premium listings.

How do I book my first listing?

Click "Book Your First Listing" or call (616) 258-4578. Send the address, pick a tier, lock the date on Calendly. 50% deposit secures the slot. Balance due before delivery.
