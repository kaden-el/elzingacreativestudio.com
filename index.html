Common Questions

FAQ

What areas does Elzinga Creative Studio serve?

We serve all of West Michigan including Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Kentwood, Walker, Grandville, Rockford, Holland, Hudsonville, and the surrounding areas across Kent and Ottawa counties. We also travel for weddings and commercial projects beyond our standard service area.

How fast will I get my real estate photos?

All real estate photography packages include 24-hour delivery. Your MLS-ready images are professionally edited with HDR processing and delivered the next day so you can get your listing live fast.

Do you offer twilight and drone photography?

Yes. We specialize in real twilight photography during blue hour — not virtual edits. Listings with real twilight photos receive up to 300% more online views. We also offer licensed Part 107 aerial drone photography for overhead property and landscape shots.

What is included in a real estate photography package?

Our Standard package ($175) includes HDR interiors and exteriors with 25-35 MLS-ready images and 24-hour delivery. Showcase ($325) adds real twilight, a 2D floor plan, social media graphics, and vertical cuts. Signature ($525) adds golden hour exteriors and a 60-second listing Reel with 55-70 images.

Do you offer video and marketing services?

Yes. Beyond photography, we produce listing walkthrough Reels, brand films, and social media video content. Our marketing services include Meta Ads, Google Ads campaign management, content creation, and Google Business Profile optimization for local businesses across Grand Rapids and West Michigan.

How do I book a session?

Fill out the contact form below, call us at (616) 258-4578, or email kaden@elzingacreativestudio.com. We typically respond within 24 hours. A 50% deposit holds your date, and the balance is due before gallery delivery.
