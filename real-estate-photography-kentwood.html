Why Kentwood listings have a different buyer profile

Kentwood is the SE Grand Rapids suburb people pick for value and access. It is one of the most diverse communities in West Michigan, sits right on the Woodland Mall and the 28th Street and East Paris retail corridor, and moves fast - well-priced homes here do not sit. The buyer pool is broad: first-time buyers, move-up families in the Kentwood Public Schools zone, investors eyeing rentals, and downsizers after a low-maintenance condo. Listings that win here lead with value and livability, not luxury.

Elzinga Creative Studio shoots Kentwood listings to match that broad buyer pool: clean, bright HDR that reads well on a phone, exterior context that shows proximity to the 28th Street and East Paris shopping and dining, and drone aerials that frame the lot and the neighborhood. In a fast market, photos that present cleanly and post the next day are what keep a Kentwood listing ahead of the comps.

Drone in Kentwood airspace

Here is the honest version: most of Kentwood sits under Gerald R. Ford International Airport (GRR) Class C controlled airspace because the city wraps right around the airport. That means commercial drone flights here almost always require LAANC authorization before takeoff - it is not optional, and a pilot without it should not be flying your listing. ECS files LAANC and flies Part 107 RPIC compliant on every aerial, so the authorization is handled before we arrive. Drone aerials add $125 a la carte and are included in the Signature tier. For the full picture see drone laws for Grand Rapids real estate agents.

Kentwood real estate photography FAQ

Do you need drone authorization to shoot Kentwood listings?
Usually, yes. Most of Kentwood falls inside GRR Class C controlled airspace, so commercial drone flights require LAANC authorization. ECS files LAANC and flies Part 107 RPIC compliant on every aerial - that step is handled for you before we arrive.

How fast do you deliver Kentwood photos?
Within 24 hours of the shoot. Kentwood moves fast, so your edited, MLS-ready gallery is back the next day and you can list without losing momentum.

What does it cost?
Three tiers: Essential $245, Showcase $375, Signature $525. Drone adds $125 and real twilight adds $150 a la carte; twilight is included in Showcase and Signature, and drone is included in Signature. Showcase fits most Kentwood listings.

Do you shoot condos and rentals, not just single-family homes?
Yes. Kentwood's housing stock is varied - ranches, condos, townhomes, rentals, and family homes - and we shoot all of it. Smaller condo and rental units fit Essential; larger family homes lean Showcase.
