In Fennville, you're selling the land as much as the house

Fennville sits in the heart of Allegan County's fruit belt, the rolling orchard-and-vineyard country a few miles inland from the Saugatuck-Douglas coast. The listings here are different from anything in the metro: farmhouses on acreage, hobby farms with outbuildings and pasture, vineyard-adjacent country homes, and rural retreats that buyers from Chicago and Grand Rapids buy precisely to get away from the subdivision grid. On a Fennville property, the land, the setting, and the privacy are usually the product — and a tight interior set alone will badly undersell it.

Elzinga Creative Studio shoots Fennville listings to do justice to the whole property. HDR keeps the farmhouse interiors warm and honest, but the drone aerial is what carries the listing: the full parcel, the orchard rows or pasture, the outbuildings, the tree line, and how the home sits in the countryside. Add real twilight and a rural Fennville listing can look like the lifestyle escape it actually is. Galleries come back within 24 hours.

Drone is non-negotiable on acreage

A farmhouse listing on ten acres has most of its value in the ten acres — and there is no ground-level photo that can show them. The drone aerial is the single image that proves the parcel size, reveals the orchard or pasture, places the outbuildings, and shows the privacy a buyer is paying for. ECS flies FAA Part 107 RPIC compliant on every shoot, and the rural Fennville area sits in Class G uncontrolled airspace, the friendliest tier for commercial drone work. Aerial is built into Signature ($495), or a $125 add-on to the lower tiers. For the rules, see drone laws for West Michigan real estate agents.

Real twilight for the rural escape

Buyers shopping Fennville are buying a feeling — the quiet, the open sky, the evening on the porch with nothing around. Real twilight photography captures exactly that: the actual blue hour with the farmhouse glowing warm against the countryside, not a fake sky pasted onto a daytime frame. On a rural retreat listing it is often the image that turns a scroll into a showing. Real twilight is included free in Showcase ($325) and Signature ($495), or add $150 to Essential.
