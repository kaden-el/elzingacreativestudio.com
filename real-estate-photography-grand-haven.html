Why Grand Haven listings have a different buyer profile

Grand Haven is a lakeshore destination first and a commuter town second. A large slice of the listings here are waterfront homes, vacation properties, and second homes, bought by buyers from Chicago, Detroit, and across the Midwest who want Lake Michigan access, the State Park beach, and the boardwalk along the Grand River channel. That buyer is purchasing a lifestyle and a location, not just a floor plan.

Elzinga Creative Studio shoots Grand Haven listings around what those buyers actually search: proximity to the lake and the channel, beach and pier access, downtown walkability to Washington Avenue, and the views that justify a waterfront premium. For a second-home buyer who may never tour in person before making an offer, the photography has to do the walkthrough. Drone aerials carry most of the weight here. They are the only way to show the water relationship, the dune setting, and how the home sits relative to the beach and the channel.

Real twilight on the lakeshore

Grand Haven faces west over Lake Michigan, which means it gets some of the best sunsets on the lake, the same sunsets people line the pier and the boardwalk to watch. Real twilight photographs capture that golden-to-blue-hour lakeshore light that a virtual edit cannot fake: warm interior glow, the deck or dock lit, water catching the last color of the day. For a waterfront listing, that one frame often becomes the hero image. Real twilight is included free in Showcase ($325) and Signature ($495) tiers, or add $150 to Essential. For the breakdown, see the real twilight service page.

Drone in Grand Haven airspace

ECS flies Part 107 RPIC compliant on every shoot and pulls LAANC authorization whenever a property falls inside controlled airspace. Grand Haven Memorial Airpark (3GM) sits just outside town, so some lakeshore and riverfront parcels need a pre-shoot airspace check before we fly, and we handle that authorization ahead of time so the shoot stays on schedule. Drone matters more in Grand Haven than almost anywhere else we work, because the water relationship is the value, and you can only show it from the air. Grand Haven listing FAQ

Do you shoot waterfront and vacation homes in Grand Haven? Yes. They are the core of this market and exactly what we build a shoot around.
