Why Allendale listings have a different buyer profile

Allendale is unusual for West Michigan: a single township that sells to two completely different buyers at once. Grand Valley State University sits at the center of town, roughly 12 miles west of Grand Rapids along Lake Michigan Drive (M-45), and its growth pulls a steady stream of student and investor rental demand. At the same time, Allendale Charter Township is one of the fastest-growing communities in Ottawa County, filling in with new family subdivisions for households who want space, the Allendale Public Schools zone, and an easy commute to both Grand Rapids and the lakeshore.

That split is the whole game. A listing photographed for a family buyer and a listing photographed for a rental investor are not the same brief. Elzinga Creative Studio shoots Allendale knowing which side of the market each property is on, and adjusts the shot list, the captions, and the aerials accordingly. Photography that markets to both families and rental investors is the real Allendale differentiator.

Drone in Allendale airspace

Most of Allendale Charter Township sits in Class G uncontrolled airspace, the friendliest tier for commercial drone work, so no LAANC authorization is needed in most cases, and ECS flies Part 107 RPIC compliant on every shoot. Where a controlled-airspace shelf does apply to a specific address, we file LAANC where required before the flight. We confirm the exact airspace for each property up front, so the aerial is legal and on schedule. For full breakdown see drone laws for Grand Rapids real estate agents.

Standard turnaround is 24 hours from shoot to edited gallery. Allendale is roughly 12 miles west of Grand Rapids along Lake Michigan Drive, so it is a core part of our service area with no travel surcharge.

Can you fly drone over properties in Allendale?
Yes. Most of Allendale Charter Township is Class G uncontrolled airspace, the friendliest tier for commercial drone work.
